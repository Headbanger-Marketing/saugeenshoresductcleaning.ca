FAQs

Straight Answers, No Guesswork

If your question is not here, it is still worth a quick call.

How often should lakeside ducts be cleaned in Saugeen Shores?

Year-round homes next to the lake do best on a two year cycle, since sand and damp arrive constantly. Seasonal cottages can stretch to three or four, ideally booked for spring opening so you spend the summer breathing clean.

Are you certified and insured?

Certified and insured on every visit, cottage or full-time home. This work has no provincial trade designation, so the training record and the liability policy are the protections that matter, and we carry both.

We only get up a few weekends a year. How do we know the work was done?

Photos of your actual ducts at the start and the end of the visit, emailed to you wherever you are, with the dates attached. Absentee owners get the same proof as standing customers.

The cottage dryer is terrible every May. Why?

A pipe that sits cold all winter collects lint, and sometimes a nest in the exterior cap. We clear the run, evict whatever moved in, and measure the airflow so the first weekend is not a laundry marathon.

What does a cleaning involve at a seasonal property?

Same full process as a house, suction at the system, registers washed, runs brushed, blower compartment cleaned, plus photos and a walkthrough or an emailed report. Most Saugeen Shores properties take three to four hours.

Can you fix the smell when the heat comes on in a closed house?

Yes, in sequence. Cleaning carries out the residue holding the odour, then a sanitizer treatment on the clean duct surfaces finishes it. It is the standard spring-opening pairing here.

Sand keeps showing up inside. Is that the ducts?

Often yes, through unsealed return ductwork in the crawl space or basement. A pressure test finds the gaps, sealing closes them, and the sand stops commuting indoors.

Do you cover Port Elgin, Southampton, and the surrounding townships?

All of Saugeen Shores plus Paisley, Tara, Chesley, and up the peninsula toward Sauble, with rural routes on request. Confirm your address when booking.

Do you serve areas outside of Saugeen Shores, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Saugeen Shores and nearby communities including Port Elgin, Southampton, Paisley, Tara, and the surrounding area.
